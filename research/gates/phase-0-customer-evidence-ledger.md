This ledger is deliberately empty until evidence is collected under the

names, contract text, credentials, or unredacted personal data belong here.

## Required row fields

Each row must have a stable pseudonymous account ID and record:

- country and organization size band;
- participant role and verified workflow ownership;
- agreement family and post-signature trigger;
- dated incident summary and exact source of evidence;
- current tools, workaround, consequence, and incumbent adequacy;
- economic owner, payer identity/role, pilot amount and payment date;
- corpus receipt status and redaction/data-handling confirmation;
- output usefulness, corrections, delivery/support minutes and stop conditions;
- second-cycle date and evidence of voluntary repeat use;
- evidence quality, reviewer, decision, and link to approved private notes.

## Evidence rules

1. Interview enthusiasm, public complaints, GitHub activity, clicks, or an
   unpaid trial do not count as payment or repeat-use evidence.
2. A pilot counts only when the same fixed scope, volume, price basis, and
   success review are recorded before delivery and payment is non-refundable.
3. A corpus counts only when it is buyer-provided, redacted or contractually
   handled, and its agreement family and provenance are recorded.
4. A repeat counts only when the organization independently requests a second
   comparable cycle; founder-initiated cleanup does not count.
5. Missing, secondhand, inferred, or conflicting evidence remains FAIL until
   corrected by a dated source. No written exception can waive a hard gate.
