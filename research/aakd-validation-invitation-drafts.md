# Aakd validation invitation drafts

**Status:** drafts only. No message has been sent. Do not address these to a named person until their current operational ownership is verified from a first-party source or a direct introduction.

## Sending rules

- Send at most one individually researched invitation per account.
- Do not state that the account has a contract problem; ask whether the workflow exists.
- Do not claim Aakd Cloud, compliance certification, customer results, automated legal advice, or integrations that are not verified.
- Stop after a “no,” no response, or evidence that the current system already covers the workflow.
- The desired outcome is a 20-minute falsification conversation—not a demo, trial, or immediate product sale.
